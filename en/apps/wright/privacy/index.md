## Information Collected

The developer of Wright does not collect any information about you through the app. Wright has no accounts, analytics, or ads.

Wright stores the following information on this device.

- Your bookmarks
- Your search history (up to 1,000 items; when there are more, the oldest unpinned items are deleted)
- Your settings

Your bookmarks and search history sync through your iCloud with devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer cannot see this data. Bookmarks and search history in Private Mode, as well as your settings, are not synced and are stored only on this device.

This information remains until you delete it as described in “Deleting Your Data.”

## Information Sent Outside the App

### Google Suggestions

To show suggestions, Wright sends the text you are typing in the search bar, along with your device’s language, to Google. This feature is on by default and also sends this information in Private Mode. To stop sending it, turn off Google suggestions in Wright’s Settings > Suggestions. This information is handled in accordance with [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

### Running a Search

When you run a search, Wright opens the site or app you chose with the words you entered. When you scan a book’s barcode, Wright searches for it on Amazon. This information is handled in accordance with each service’s privacy policy. Web pages opened inside Wright share cookies with Safari.

### Clipboard

Wright reads the text on the clipboard only when you tap the paste button in the search bar, or when you search with a bookmark set up to use clipboard text.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** Used to scan QR codes and book barcodes.
- **Media & Apple Music:** Used to search with the title of the song that is playing. Requested the first time you search with a bookmark set up to use it.
- **Biometrics:** Used to unlock Private Mode with Face ID or Touch ID.

You can use the rest of the app without allowing any of these. You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Deleting Your Data

- To delete a single item from your search history or bookmarks, touch and hold it, then choose Delete.
- In Wright’s Settings, use Delete Data to delete the bookmarks, search history, or all data in the mode you’re viewing (normal or Private).
- Deleting Wright deletes the information stored on this device. Bookmarks and search history synced to iCloud remain, so delete them inside Wright before you delete the app.

## Contact Form

Messages you send using the contact form on the [Support](../support/) page are received by the developer. The form does not collect your name, email address, or other contact information. The developer uses these messages only to improve the apps.

Because the form uses Google Forms, messages you send are also handled in accordance with [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).
