Type a few letters, then pick the site or app to search with. Wright makes everyday searching a little easier.
{: .lead}

Wright is an app made just to help you search. The keyboard is ready as soon as you open it, suggestions appear as you type, and you can search right away with the sites you use most.

## Key Features

### Start typing right away

The keyboard appears as soon as you open Wright. The search bar sits at the bottom of the screen, so you can type with one hand.

### Pick from suggestions

As you type, Wright suggests items from your search history, Google suggestions, calculator results, and text on the clipboard. Even words you can’t quite remember are easy to find.

### Switch search sites with bookmarks

Keep the sites you search most, like Google, YouTube, Wikipedia, X, and Amazon, as bookmarks. Search the web and social media for the same words, one after another.

### Private Mode

Keeps your search history and bookmarks separate from your usual ones, stored only on this device. You can also protect it with Face ID or Touch ID.

### Scan a code to search

Scan a QR code or a book’s barcode to search for it.

### Sync with iCloud

Your bookmarks and search history sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Great For

- Search bars at the top of the browser that are hard to reach
- Searching again for something you looked up yesterday
- Searching the web, then social media, for the same words
- Apps that each put their search button in a different place
- Words you can’t quite remember

## Privacy

The developer doesn’t collect any information about you. To show suggestions, Wright sends what you type to Google. If you don’t want that, turn it off in Wright’s Settings > Suggestions.
