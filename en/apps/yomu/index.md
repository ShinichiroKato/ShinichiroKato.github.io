Yomu is an RSS reader app. When you open an article, Yomu extracts the article text from its page and shows it.

## Key Features

### Subscribe with a link

Paste a site’s address, or share a page to Yomu from Safari or another app, and Yomu finds its feed. You can import your subscriptions from another reader with OPML.

### New articles, automatically

New articles come in automatically in the background, with unread counts for each folder and feed. You can also turn on notifications for new articles, feed by feed.

### Read articles

Choose the background, text size, line spacing, and font. Embedded posts and videos appear as cards. Yomu can also read articles aloud.

### Summaries and translation

With Apple Intelligence, Yomu sums up an article in 3 to 5 bullet points. Articles in other languages are translated into your device’s language, along with their titles in the article list.

### Organize with folders

Along with folders, create smart folders by conditions, like “Unread today.” Add muted words for topics you’d rather skip. Lock folders with biometric authentication.

### On your device

Extracting, translating, and summarizing articles all happen on your device. Yomu gets feeds directly from each site, so you don’t need an account with an RSS service.

### Sync with iCloud

Your subscriptions and read status sync across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account, using iCloud.

## Great For

- Checking what’s new from the favorite sites you chose
- Reading overseas news and blogs in your language

## Good to Know

- Translation works for the languages supported by Apple’s translation feature, and requires the language to be downloaded on your device.
- Summaries require a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
- The article can’t be extracted from some pages, such as members-only articles.
- Article text and images load when you open them, so they aren’t available offline. The article list is.
- Feeds that aren’t available over HTTPS aren’t supported.
