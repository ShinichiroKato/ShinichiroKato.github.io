## Information Collected

The developer of Yomu does not collect any information about you through the app, except as described in “Contact Form.” Yomu has no accounts, analytics, or ads.

Yomu stores the following information on this device and syncs it across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account, using iCloud.

- The feeds you subscribe to, and each feed’s article settings
- Folders (including whether they’re locked) and smart folders
- Muted words
- Article titles, feed excerpts, read and starred status, and translated titles
- The Keep Read Articles setting

The following information is stored only on this device and not synced.

- Other settings
- Whether new article notifications are on for each feed

If you aren’t signed in to iCloud, or iCloud is turned off for Yomu, the information above isn’t synced either and is stored only on this device. Article text, images, translations, and summaries are kept only while you’re using the app and aren’t saved on your device. Extracting article text, translating, and summarizing all happen on your device.

Stored information remains until you delete it as described in “Deleting Your Data.” It may also be included in your device backups.

If you turn on Share With App Developers, the developer may receive crash logs and diagnostic information from Apple. Apple provides this information in a form that doesn’t identify you. It is used to fix bugs.

## Information Sent Outside the App

- **The sites you subscribe to:** When feeds update, when you open an article, and when the article list is shown, Yomu fetches feeds, article pages, images in articles, pages linked from articles (to show link cards), and thumbnails and site icons from the sites that publish them. Those sites may learn that the content was fetched, and may also receive your IP address. Yomu doesn’t send or accept cookies. This information is handled in accordance with each site’s privacy policy.
- **X:** When an article links to or embeds a post on X, Yomu sends the post’s identifier to X to show the post. X may also receive your IP address. This information is handled in accordance with [X’s Privacy Policy](https://x.com/en/privacy).
- **YouTube:** When an article links to or embeds a YouTube video, Yomu sends the video’s identifier to YouTube to show its title and thumbnail. YouTube may also receive your IP address. This information is handled in accordance with [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).
- **iCloud:** Yomu sends the information listed in “Information Collected” to Apple to sync it across your devices. This information is not sent to the developer and is handled in accordance with [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** Used for notifications about new articles and to show the number of unread articles on the app icon.
- **Biometrics:** Used to authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID when you open a locked folder.

If you don’t allow notifications, new article notifications and the unread count on the app icon aren’t shown, but you can still use the rest of the app. If you don’t allow biometrics, you authenticate with your passcode or password instead. You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Deleting Your Data

- When you unsubscribe from a feed, the feed and its articles (including starred articles) are deleted from all of your devices using iCloud.
- Settings > Reset Yomu > Delete All Feeds in Yomu deletes all feeds, folders, and articles from all of your devices using iCloud. Settings, muted words, and smart folders aren’t deleted.
- You can delete muted words and smart folders one at a time.
- You can restore settings to their defaults with Reset All Settings.
- Read articles are deleted automatically after the number of days in Keep Read Articles.
- Deleting Yomu deletes the information stored on this device. Information synced with iCloud remains after you delete Yomu. To delete it, before you delete Yomu, use Delete All Feeds and delete your muted words and smart folders.

## Contact Form

Messages you send using the contact form on the [Support](../support/) page are received by the developer. The form has no fields for your name, email address, or other contact information. The developer uses these messages to improve the apps and does not share them with third parties except as required by law. Messages that are no longer needed are deleted. Because the form can’t identify who sent a message, individual messages can’t be deleted after they are sent.

If you open the form from Settings > About Yomu > Send Feedback in Yomu, the app’s name (Yomu) is filled in. If you open it from Report an Issue in an article’s … menu, the app’s name, the article’s URL, the version of Yomu, and the OS version are filled in. In both cases, the developer receives this information when you send the form. This information is sent to Google when the form opens.

Because the form uses Google Forms, messages you send are also handled in accordance with [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).
